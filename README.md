# Douglas Clark

Entrepreneur, researcher, and systems thinker. I design systems that reveal meaning hidden inside complex information: cryptologic analysis at NSA, satellite telemetry, enterprise knowledge graphs, and now AI. Across each domain, the same pattern holds: information is defined not by its storage or presentation, but by the structure of relationships within it.

## Research

- **[psa-research](https://github.com/fmertz55/psa-research)** — Anchor-triangulated semantic signatures (PSA). At scale, I could not compare every sentence with every other for paraphrase detection, so I needed to narrow the neighborhood. PSA measures text against a fixed catalog of propositional anchor sentences ("guide stars") and adds a calibrated index for semantic blocking. [Preprint (PDF)](https://github.com/fmertz55/psa-research/blob/main/paper/latex/Clark_PSA_anchor_signatures_preprint.pdf) · patent pending
- **[iota](https://github.com/fmertz55/iota)** — An open lab notebook for one idea: information comes first, and what we call physics is how information is kept, moved, and constrained. Tested in simple computational worlds where every cell and rule is known exactly. *The Shadow Knows* is under review at *Complex Systems*.
- **[ICP](https://github.com/fmertz55/ICP)** — The Information-Constraint Principle: information is the physically embodied way an interaction changes what can happen next. A structured literature review with an adversarial verdict on what is and isn't established; the hypothesis paper is under review at *BioSystems*.

## How I work

I direct much of this research through AI agents, under preregistration. Every experiment's question, method, and pass/fail criteria are frozen as a git tag before it runs. Artifacts are hash-sealed, failed and invalid runs stay in the record with their explanations, and results face adversarial audits. The old NSA analyst never forgot how to find the cribs.
